HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the entire ZIP into one folder.
2. Keep index.html, photo1.jpg–photo5.jpg, song.mp3, and sherni-song.mp3 together in that same folder.
3. Open index.html in a modern browser. For the best phone experience, open the extracted folder in a browser or upload the complete folder to a static website host.

MUSIC
• song.mp3 = soft background music, loops at a low volume.
• sherni-song.mp3 = special song started by the “Press play, my Sherni 💗” button.
• The special song pauses the background music so they do not overlap. When the special song ends, background music resumes if it had been playing.
• Phones may block autoplay; tap “Open your surprise” or the background music button.

Keep all files together. Do not rename the photo or music files unless you also update index.html.
